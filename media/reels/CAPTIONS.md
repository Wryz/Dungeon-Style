# DungeonStyle reels: ready to post

Every reel comes in two versions:

- **`ReelN….mp4`**: royalty-free music baked in, with every cut landing on its beat. **Post this one** unless you want a trending sound.
- **`ReelN…-game-sound.mp4`**: same edit with game sound only (hits, crunches, crowd). Use it to add a trending song from Instagram's audio picker. The cuts follow the baked track's tempo, so a different song will be close but not frame-exact.

All files are 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, mastered to about −14 LUFS (Instagram's playback level), and 19–21 s long. Each one ends on a flash that loops back into the opening. Covers are `CoverN….jpg`, with the title kept in the centre so it survives the profile-grid crop.

### Posting tips
- Post one reel a day or every other day rather than all three at once. Reel 1 is the strongest opener.
- Pin a comment with the link (or "link in bio 🎮") and reply to early comments. The comment-bait lines above are there to get people talking.
- Music credits: the two Alex Morgan tracks are CC BY 4.0, so keep the credit line in the caption. "Ai Contact" is CC0, so its credit is optional.
- Trending songs are copyrighted, so add them only through Instagram's audio picker, never baked into the file.
